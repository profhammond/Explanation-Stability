# Claim Boundaries

## Authorized statements

- EDI quantifies paired attribution-map stability using correlation and structural-drift components.
- In the evaluated ISIC-100 fixed-model analysis, EDI was not detectably associated with deterioration in deletion AUC or soft IoU.
- The deletion-faithfulness association conclusion was unchanged across the evaluated substrates and step counts.
- Absolute deletion-AUC values varied by replacement substrate.
- PH2 served as an auxiliary external localization replication and showed method-dependent associations.
- Selected faithfulness and localization metrics responded to deterministic negative controls, but control behavior was mixed across metrics.

## Statements not authorized

- EDI measures explanation correctness, causality, or clinical validity.
- Lower EDI identifies a superior preprocessing method.
- The absence of an association proves that stability and explanation quality are unrelated.
- PH2 constitutes prospective, institutional, or clinical validation.
- Lesion-mask overlap identifies every diagnostically relevant image region.
- A universal or clinical EDI threshold has been established.
- Image-clustered intervals are equivalent to patient-clustered intervals.

## Required qualifiers

Results are bounded to the evaluated frozen classifier, cohorts, attribution methods, transformations, perturbation protocols, masks, metrics, and clustering unit. Method-specific and cohort-specific estimates should be retained rather than replaced with an unconditional pooled conclusion.
